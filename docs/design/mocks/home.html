How it works

Upload calls, find any of them in seconds, ask about the one you need.

  1. 1

    Upload or import

    Drop in audio, transcripts or a whole folder. Audio without a transcript is transcribed, and duplicates are skipped so nothing is transcribed twice.

  2. 2

    Search by who, what or intent

    Find calls by contact, date, exact or fuzzy keyword, tag, or meaning: “people interested in buying” finds them even if nobody said “buy”.

  3. 3

    Ask about any call

    Ask what was promised or what price you quoted, and get an answer that points to the moment in the call.

Privacy you can check

Your device encrypts. Our server only stores what it can’t read.

Your device

  • Transcripts, audio, names, notes
  • Your keys, unlocked by passkey
  • Search terms and decrypted results
Only your device sees plaintext

AskCalls server

  • Ciphertext and wrapped keys
  • Keyed HMAC tokens for words and contacts
  • Vectors of redacted text, day, duration bucket

Storage

  • Postgres with pgvector, in the EU
  • Encrypted recordings in an S3 bucket
  • Or your own bucket, signed by your device

Where plaintext does go, and only when you ask

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is recording calls legal?

It depends on where you and the other person are. Israel and many US states allow recording a call you take part in; others require everyone’s consent. AskCalls stores calls you already have; it doesn’t record them. Check the law that applies to you.

What if I lose my phone?

Unlock on another device with your passkey (if it syncs) or your recovery code. If you lose every passkey and the recovery code, your calls can’t be recovered: we never had your keys.

Can AskCalls staff see my calls?

No. We store ciphertext and keyed tokens. Plaintext reaches a third party only when you ask: audio to the transcription provider, and one call to the AI provider when you ask about it.

Where is my data stored?

On askcalls.com, in Postgres and an S3-compatible bucket in the EU. You can keep recordings in your own bucket, or run everything on your own computer with askcalls local.

Can I self-host?

Yes. Run docker compose up, and a setup wizard walks you through the database, storage, sign-in and providers. It can run fully offline.
