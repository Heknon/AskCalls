    Questions people
    ask first.

    Is recording calls legal?

    It depends on where you and the other person are. Many places allow recording a call you take part in; some require everyone on the call to agree. Check the rules where you work. AskCalls doesn't record calls; it stores what you upload.

    What if I lose my phone?

    Unlock on another device with your passkey, if it syncs, or with your recovery code. If you lose every passkey and the recovery code, your calls can't be recovered: we never had your keys.

    Can AskCalls staff see my calls?

    No. We store ciphertext and keyed tokens. Without your keys, which never reach us, there is nothing to read.

    Where is my data stored?

    In Postgres and an S3 bucket in the EU, encrypted on your device before upload. Recording backups can go to your own bucket instead.

    Can I self-host?

    Yes. Run docker compose up and a setup wizard walks you through the database, storage, sign-in and providers. It can run fully offline.
