The limits

Beta limits, per account on askcalls.com

  • 500callsA call is one transcript, with or without a recording. Deleting a call frees its place.
  • 300transcription minutes a monthCounted by audio length. Transcripts you upload ready-made don't count.
  • 2GBof recording backupsEncrypted and compressed on your device. With your own bucket, there is no limit from us.
  • 50questions a dayOne question about one call. Resets at midnight.
  • 5access tokensFor the local agent, remote MCP and scripts. Each token has its own scopes.
  • 60MCP fetches an hour, per tokenCiphertext fetches over remote MCP. The local agent decrypts on your machine.

What counts.

  • Your own keys don't count.

    Transcription and questions with your own provider key go straight from your device to the provider and don't count toward the limits.

  • Duplicates aren't transcribed twice.

    The same call, even re-encoded, is recognised by its audio fingerprint and skipped before any minutes count.

  • Search and reading are always free.

    They run on your device, so they have no limit.

At the limit, uploads pause until you delete calls or the month resets. Search and reading keep working.

Self-hosted.
Your limits.

AskCalls is open source under MIT. On your own instance the admin sets the limits, the providers and where data lives, and it can run with no internet at all.

The self-hosting guide
~/askcalls
$ git clone https://github.com/askcalls/askcalls
$ docker compose up -d
  postgres   healthy
  minio      healthy
  web        ready on http://localhost:3000
  Open /setup to finish in the browser.

Pricing
questions.

What happens at the limit?

Uploads pause until you delete calls or the month resets. Search, reading, playback and export keep working, and nothing is deleted.

What about my own keys?

Transcription and questions with your own key don't count toward the limits. The key is encrypted with your keys and used only on your device.

Is self-hosting free?

Yes. The code is MIT-licensed. You pay only for the server, storage and providers you choose, or nothing if it all runs locally.
